One-Click Job Autofill

1. Local résumé parser

The parser only needs to be running when you choose to extract details from a new résumé. Once saved, your profile and original résumé stay in this browser, and one-click autofill does not need a terminal or parser. No résumé or form data is sent to an AI provider.

2. Your résumé

Checking saved résumé...

The original file (up to 10 MB) can be saved and uploaded as-is in any format. Local text extraction supports PDF, DOCX, ODT, HTML, TXT and MD.

3. Review your profile

The profile starts with neutral, blank personal details and can be populated from your CV. Add only details you want to use, and review them before saving. Answers you enter manually on application forms are learned and saved locally against their question, so a matching or closely related question can be filled next time. You can review or remove learned answers in the answers object below. Unprovided sensitive answers, such as citizenship, stay blank for you to answer.
